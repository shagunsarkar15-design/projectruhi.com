THE WORLD WE SEE

Every place has
a different story.

Children grow up in different places, surrounded by different opportunities, challenges, communities and experiences. We want to understand those stories before we decide how we can contribute.

01 — Delhi

Learning access

Explore how children experience learning, opportunity and community.

02 — Jaipur

Creativity & expression

Making space for children to express, create and imagine.

03 — Mumbai

Community

Understanding the people and spaces that shape childhood.

04 — Kolkata

Social knowledge

Helping children understand the world around them.

05 — Bengaluru

Learning differently

Exploring new ways for children to learn and participate.

06 — Chennai

Opportunity

Looking at what helps children imagine possibilities for themselves.
