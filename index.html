01 / ABOUT रूhī

Childhood should have room for curiosity.

We believe education is more than what happens inside a classroom.


It is having someone answer your questions. It is discovering something new. It is knowing about the world around you. It is having the confidence to say, "I want to try."


रूhī works with children and communities to create opportunities for learning, social awareness and expression.

02 / THE WORLD WE SEE

India is many stories.
